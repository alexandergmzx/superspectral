# 05 — Academic papers

The reading list that establishes prior art, fixes the definitions the firmware and the host implement, and supplies the citations for the proposal, the validation plan, and the eventual preprint.

**Organised by topic**, and the topic slugs are exactly the directories of the reference library under [`../papers/by-topic/`](../papers/README.md) — a paper's section here *is* its filing location. Entry numbers are append-only; cite an entry as `05 #<n>` from anywhere in the repo.

§ numbers in the **Why** column refer to the [proposal](../proposal/01-super-spectral-proposal.md) (§1 motivation/RQ · §2 objectives O1–O5 · §3 technical approach · §4 validation · §5 contributions · §7 limitations); ADR numbers refer to the [ADR index and backlog](../adr/README.md); metric names are the rows of the [validation plan](../validation/README.md); experiment 0001 is the PDM-mic in-situ characterisation recipe under [`../validation/experiments/`](../validation/experiments/README.md); component names (`spectral_core`, `spectral_fft_backend`, `audio_source`, `display_backend`, `twatch_bsp`, `ui`) are the firmware components under [`firmware/twatch-s3/`](../../firmware/twatch-s3/README.md).

Columns: **Identifier** = DOI / arXiv ID / stable URL (a DOI always resolves at `https://doi.org/<DOI>`). **OA** = ✅ free full text at the publisher, arXiv, a proceedings archive, or the author's own page · ⚠️ a free copy exists but on an unofficial mirror, or the link is unverified · — paywalled (DOI + institutional access, or email the corresponding author). **Priority**: ★★★ must-have/blocking · ★★ strongly recommended · ★ useful background.

If you can only read a dozen papers before the first line of DSP code, read these, in this order:
