# 0011 — The default spectrogram colormap is cividis, and its LUT is designed *in* RGB565 rather than converted to it

- **Status:** proposed — this is a **perception and design judgement**, not an engineering fact. The quantisation machinery below is settled and measured; the choice of default map, and whether dithering is on, are the author's to make on the panel, in daylight, on his own voice. They are stated under *What the author must decide*.
- **Date:** 2026-08-21
- **Context:** The spectrogram **is** the product ([proposal §3](../proposal/01-super-spectral-proposal.md)), and it is read on a 1.3″ 240×240 ST7789V3 driven **RGB565 over SPI** ([`../hw/twatch-s3-pins.md`](../hw/twatch-s3-pins.md)), on a wrist, at arm's length, sometimes outdoors, by a singer who is not allowed to touch it ([ADR 0012](0012-hands-free-interaction.md)). Two literatures bear on that and they do not overlap:
  1. **Which map.** Rainbow/jet maps introduce luminance reversals and false boundaries and are rejected in the visualisation literature ([05 #80](../bibliography/05-papers.md), [05 #77](../bibliography/05-papers.md)); the fix is a perceptually uniform, luminance-monotone sequential map ([05 #78](../bibliography/05-papers.md), [05 #82](../bibliography/05-papers.md), [05 #79](../bibliography/05-papers.md)), of which **cividis** is the one explicitly optimised for colour-vision deficiency at constant linear luminance ([05 #76](../bibliography/05-papers.md)). The caveat is empirical: no single map wins every task, and peak-reading and texture-reading pull in different directions ([05 #81](../bibliography/05-papers.md)).
  2. **The 5/6/5 problem, which none of those papers is about.** Every one of those maps is published as 8-bit sRGB. This panel shows **32 red levels, 64 green, 32 blue**. Quantising a perceptually uniform ramp to that grid collapses it into flat runs of identical words, and *the boundary between two runs is a contour line the signal does not contain*. In a spectrogram that reads as structure — the one artefact a measurement instrument may not invent. The reference projects do not address it at all: xiao-edge-audio hand-rolls a 4-stop gradient and the vendor factory example a `(dB+40)/40` scale ([ADR 0018](0018-first-reference-project-study.md), [notes](../reference-projects/notes/xiao-edge-audio_notes.md)), neither perceptually uniform nor RGB565-aware.
  The controller offers one escape: `COLMOD (3Ah)` selects 16-bit/65K (`05h`, 2 bytes/pixel) or 18-bit/262K (`06h`, 3 bytes/pixel) — ST7789V3 preliminary V0.0 §§8.7.3/8.7.4, [01 #13](../bibliography/01-datasheets.md) — and `RAMCTRL (B0h)` `EPF[1:0]` (p. 220) chooses how a 65K pixel's missing red/blue LSB is filled when it is expanded into the panel's 6-bit frame memory. The 18-bit path costs 1.5× the pixel bytes on the same SPI bus.
- **Decision (proposed):**
  1. **Default map: `cividis`.** Reasons in the order they matter for *this* device: it is CVD-optimised by construction rather than by inspection ([05 #76](../bibliography/05-papers.md)) — and a real-time visual voice display is assistive technology, so that is a functional requirement, not a courtesy ([ADR 0012](0012-hands-free-interaction.md) item 6, [09 §E](../bibliography/09-visual-feedback-for-singing.md)); its lightness is monotone and near-linear, so it degrades gracefully to the one channel that survives sunlight and a phone photograph of the screen (measured on our LUT: **L\* 12.8 → 92.1**, non-decreasing); and it is a **two-hue** blue→yellow ramp, which is the axis that stays separable under all three common dichromacies, so hue carries no information that luminance does not already carry.
  2. **`viridis` and `magma` ship selectable; `batlow` when its table is vendored; a rainbow map never ships.** The map is a preset field ([ADR 0010](0010-preset-schema.md)) — one byte naming a LUT, not a per-preset palette to hand-tune.
  3. **The LUT is 256 entries and is *designed in* RGB565, not converted to it.** [`../../python-scripts/gen_colormap_lut.py`](../../python-scripts/gen_colormap_lut.py) picks the sequence of representable 565 words minimising total CIE76 ΔE to the published map **subject to a hard non-decreasing L\* constraint** (an exact shortest-path/DP solution, not a greedy round), plus a 0.5 ΔE penalty per repeated index that breaks flat runs where breaking them is nearly free. Measured, all three maps, `--self-test`:

     | map | conversion | distinct words / 256 | longest flat run | worst step ΔE76 | worst error vs the 8-bit map | L\* monotone |
     |---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|:--:|
     | cividis | **designed in 565** | 75 | 10 | **5.82** | 2.91 | yes |
     | cividis | naive per-channel rounding | 93 | 6 | 8.76 | 3.89 | **no** (8 drops, worst 0.17 L\*) |
     | cividis | 18-bit RGB666 (reference) | 130 | 5 | 3.92 | 1.88 | yes |
     | viridis | designed in 565 | 93 | 6 | 5.10 | 2.56 | yes |
     | magma | designed in 565 | 112 | 6 | 6.74 | 3.56 | yes |

     The **worst step** is the banding metric: it is the perceptual size of the edge a smooth ramp will draw. The **distinct count** is the honest one: cividis has 75 distinguishable colours on this panel, not 256, and the naive conversion buys 18 more of them at the price of a 50 % larger worst edge and a non-monotone lightness ramp.
  4. **The spectrogram history ring stores the 8-bit LUT index, never the colour.** Colour is applied at blit time. That halves the PSRAM history (57.6 KB vs 115.2 KB per 240×240 screenful), lets the map be changed retroactively over already-captured history, and keeps the take format free of any display concern ([`protocols/specs/`](../../protocols/specs/README.md)).
  5. **Ordered (4×4 Bayer) dithering is implemented, documented and shipped *off* by default.** It perturbs the **index**, not the colour, by up to half the flat run it lands in (`--dither` emits the matrix, a per-index amplitude table and the lookup helper), so it acts exactly where the LUT is flat and nowhere else. It must be keyed to the **panel** y — the ST7789 scroll address — not the buffer row, or the tile crawls with the waterfall ([ADR 0007](README.md), backlog).
  6. **18-bit `COLMOD` is the escape hatch, not the plan.** Its cost, computed from 240×240 and the two byte-per-pixel figures (bus time only — no command, DMA-gap or CS overhead): a full frame is **115 200 B → 46.08 ms at 20 MHz** (21.7 Hz ceiling) in RGB565 versus **172 800 B → 69.12 ms** (14.5 Hz) in RGB666; 50 Hz full-frame needs **46.1 MHz** versus **69.1 MHz**. If ADR 0007's hardware scroll verifies and the waterfall writes one column per frame, the same choice is **480 B → 192 µs** versus **720 B → 288 µs** at 20 MHz, which is affordable — so the verdict is *deferred on ADR 0007*, not closed.
  7. **Colour is never the only channel.** Peak markers, the f0 numeral and the clipping flag carry the same information without hue ([03 #23](../bibliography/03-standards.md), [09 S3](../bibliography/09-visual-feedback-for-singing.md)); this holds whatever D1 decides.
  8. **The generated header stays a build artefact until the licence question in this decision is closed.** Once it is, the header becomes a committed artefact whose banner carries its own metrics, and a CI job regenerates and diffs it so the table above cannot quietly stop being true. **Neither the committed header nor that job exists yet** — writing them is part of accepting this record (see the consequence at the bottom of this file). The colour tables' licence is an open ADR 0004 item recorded in the generator: matplotlib's terms cover the copy we read, the cividis table originates in a CC BY 4.0 paper, and the viridis family was released CC0 — the exact terms must reach `NOTICE` before a generated header is linked into firmware ([ADR 0004](0004-split-licensing.md), [06 #37](../bibliography/06-reference-projects.md)).
  9. **Scope.** This record owns *index → colour*. The mapping *dBFS → index* (range, floor, any knee) belongs to ADR 0006 and the preset schema ([ADR 0010](0010-preset-schema.md)); the canvas and the scroll belong to ADR 0007.

- **What the author must decide** (nothing below is decidable from a paper; each needs the panel, and D1 and D2 need his own voice on it):
  - **D1 — the default map.** cividis (proposed) vs viridis vs batlow. The literature is not unanimous and says so: [05 #81](../bibliography/05-papers.md) finds the best map is *task*-dependent, and this display serves two tasks at once — reading a peak (formant, ring band) and reading texture (vibrato, harmonic structure, noise). Decide by rendering the same take through all three on the panel, indoors and in sun, and doing both tasks.
  - **D2 — dithering on or off by default, per preset or globally.** It trades one visible contour for spatial grain. On a 1.3″ panel at arm's length, grain may itself read as noise in the signal — the opposite of the goal — and it makes every screenshot regression noisy. An A/B on hardware decides it; there is no defensible armchair answer.
  - **D3 — whether to spend SPI bandwidth on 18-bit colour**, and if so only in the scrolled-column path. Blocked on ADR 0007's scroll-axis verification; the numbers are in decision 6.
  - **D4 — the `EPF[1:0]` setting**, which decides how the panel fills the missing red/blue LSB and therefore where the top of the ramp actually lands. The LUT must be measured against whichever value is programmed. *(The V3 OCR of that table loses a column: the exact fill rule for `EPF=10`/`11` is **(verify)** against the filed PDF, [01 #13](../bibliography/01-datasheets.md) p. 220 — `00` and `01` fill 0 and 1 respectively.)*
  - **D5 — the run-breaking penalty (`--repeat-penalty`, default 0.5 ΔE).** It is the one aesthetic knob in the generator: 0 gives the pure fidelity optimum (56 distinct words for cividis), 0.5 gives 75 at no measurable cost in worst step or worst error, 2.0 gives 133 but a worse worst step. Pick a value once, by looking.
- **Alternatives:**
  - *Rainbow / jet / the Spectroid-style palette users recognise.* **Rejected** ([05 #80](../bibliography/05-papers.md), [05 #77](../bibliography/05-papers.md)): luminance reversals invent boundaries, and this device exists to measure. Reopens only if a validation session shows that a two-hue map costs peak-reading accuracy — and even then the answer is a *different* uniform map, not a rainbow.
  - *viridis as the default.* **Deferred, not rejected** (D1). It is the better-known map and the derivation reference for the method ([05 #78](../bibliography/05-papers.md)). Trigger: the peak-reading task in the O3 sessions.
  - *batlow.* **Deferred** until its table is vendored with a recorded version and licence ([06 #37](../bibliography/06-reference-projects.md)); the generator refuses `--map batlow` with that message rather than approximating it. Trigger: the table lands in the tree.
  - *Greyscale, the Visible Speech tradition* ([04 #9](../bibliography/04-books.md)). **Rejected as the default** — it discards the panel's only advantage over a printed spectrogram and roughly halves the distinguishable steps — but **kept as a selectable map**, because it is maximally CVD-safe and print-honest. Trigger: if the panel's colour rendering in daylight measures worse than its luminance rendering.
  - *Convert the published 8-bit map by per-channel rounding (what everyone does).* **Rejected** with the numbers in decision 3: a 50 % larger worst edge and a lightness ramp that is no longer monotone.
  - *Error-diffusion dithering (Floyd–Steinberg).* **Rejected**: it is serial across pixels, which does not fit a column-at-a-time waterfall with hardware scroll, and it makes a frame depend on its neighbours' history, which destroys reproducible screenshot tests ([ADR 0013](0013-native-linux-simulator-target.md)). Reopens only if the canvas ever becomes a full-frame blit *and* ordered dithering has already failed D2.
  - *A shorter LUT (32 or 64 entries) to save flash.* **Rejected**: distinct colours are already the binding constraint (75 of 256), so a shorter LUT bands harder; 512 B is not the resource under pressure.
  - *Generate the LUT on the device at boot.* **Rejected**: it puts a colour-appearance model in the firmware to save 512 B, and it makes the panel's colours a function of the build rather than a committed artefact a reviewer can diff.
- **Consequences:**
  - (+) The default is CVD-safe and luminance-monotone *by construction*, and both properties are asserted in CI rather than assumed.
  - (+) Quantisation is a measured quantity with a regenerating command behind it: worst edge ΔE 5.82 against the naive conversion's 8.76, and 3.92 stated for the 18-bit path we did not take.
  - (+) Storing indices rather than colours halves the history footprint and makes D1 reversible after the fact, including over already-recorded history.
  - (−) **Only 75 of 256 entries are distinct colours.** On an 80 dB display range that is 0.31 dB per index *(prov., the range is ADR 0006's)*, so the widest flat run shows a ≈ 3.1 dB span as one colour. Dithering hides the edge; it does not restore the resolution.
  - (−) A two-hue map has less hue variety for texture reading than viridis or magma, and [05 #81](../bibliography/05-papers.md) is explicit that the best map depends on the task. If D1 goes to cividis, that cost is accepted deliberately.
  - (−) Dithering, if D2 turns it on, adds a multiply and two table reads per pixel in the hot render loop, must track the ST7789 scroll address to avoid crawling, and makes photographed screenshots grainy — including the ones used as evidence.
  - (−) The colour tables' licence is unresolved (decision 8); until it is, the generated header is a build artefact, not a committed one.
  - (−) Every judgement here is being made about a panel that has never been photometered: the ≈ 450 cd/m² figure in circulation has **no source** ([09 §C](../bibliography/09-visual-feedback-for-singing.md), and [ADR 0012](0012-hands-free-interaction.md) records the same gap), and no outdoor condition has been run.
  - (−) Kovesi's sine-ramp acceptance test ([05 #79](../bibliography/05-papers.md)) — the standard way to *see* residual banding — has not been run; it needs the simulator target ([ADR 0013](0013-native-linux-simulator-target.md)) or a photograph of the panel.
  - (−) The generator embeds control points at stride 4 rather than the full 256-entry tables (max reconstruction error ΔE 0.375 for cividis, measured), so the LUT is faithful to a *compressed* copy of the published map. The error is an order below one 565 step, but it is not zero.
  - Flagged as a validation item **on acceptance**: the two rows below are appended to [`../validation/README.md`](../validation/README.md) as a `### Display metrics (per ADR 0011)` block when this record leaves `proposed` — deliberately with `TBD` targets, because a legibility threshold invented at a desk is exactly the kind of number this project refuses.
