# 0012 — Nothing on the path of a take needs a finger: arming and mode changes are hands-free (BMA423 wrist-raise + DRV2605L haptic confirm), and touch is a setup-only input

- **Status:** proposed — this is a **UX judgement**, not an engineering fact. The hardware primitives below are settled; the gesture set, the timings and the voice question are not, and are stated for the author under *What the author must decide*.
- **Date:** 2026-08-21
- **Context:** The device is a measurement instrument worn on the limb whose position is the measurement. **You cannot touch the watch while singing.** A finger on a 1.3″ screen mid-phrase does four things at once: it moves the microphone inside the very wrist-position envelope the validation plan reports as a surface ([`../validation/README.md`](../validation/README.md), *Wrist-position envelope*); it puts a hand between the mouth and the acoustic port; it couples a mechanical transient into a PDM MEMS microphone that shares a PCB with the touch panel; and it costs the singer the phrase. The founding research document and the proposal describe *what is displayed* and never *how it is operated* — the completeness critique §I (gitignored, `scratch/research/critic.md`) names this as an untouched gap, and the bibliography opened a whole thematic section for it ([09 §D](../bibliography/09-visual-feedback-for-singing.md), "Glanceable, hands-free interaction"). The board already carries the primitives: a **BMA423** at `0x19` on I²C0 with **INT1 on GPIO14**, whose Bosch feature engine provides tilt/wake as engine features rather than hand-rolled maths; a **DRV2605L** at `0x5A` whose enable is the AXP2101 **BLDO2** rail (there is no GPIO enable); and an **FT6336U** at `0x38` on the separate I²C1 bus ([`../hw/twatch-s3-pins.md`](../hw/twatch-s3-pins.md); all five addresses answered at the E1 gate). Two constraints bound anything written here: the anti-brick sleep gate ([ADR 0015](0015-anti-brick-policy.md) item 5) owns every path into light or deep sleep, and there is no radio in v1 ([ADR 0017](0017-no-radio-in-v1-trimmed-component-set.md)), so no phone can act as the remote control. Finally, this is where the project records that a **real-time visual voice display is assistive technology** for deaf and hard-of-hearing singers and for speech training ([05 #75](../bibliography/05-papers.md), Öster 2006) — a design constraint that has to be written down before the first screen is drawn, not audited afterwards.

- **Decision (proposed):**
  1. **Touch is a setup-only input and is never on the path of a take.** The FT6336U serves preset selection, settings, take review and the frequency-axis pinch ([01 #16](../bibliography/01-datasheets.md)) — all of it *between* phrases. Every function that must happen while the singer is phonating has a non-touch path, and the take state machine consumes no touch event once its state leaves `IDLE`. This is testable without hardware: the state machine is a `spectral_core`/`ui` object that runs in the native-Linux simulator ([ADR 0013](0013-native-linux-simulator-target.md)).
  2. **Arming is a wrist raise, through the Bosch feature engine — not through arithmetic of our own.** `BMA423_TILT` (`0x10`) is enabled with its interrupt mapped to INT1 → **GPIO14**; the ISR posts to a queue and the UI task services it. Two configuration facts are normative, both from the [SensorLib study notes](../reference-projects/notes/sensorlib_notes.md) §4.3: the 6 144-byte `bma423_config_file` blob must be uploaded (without it there is no tilt engine at all), and **`selectPlatform(Platform::WRISTBAND)` must be called explicitly** — nothing in `bma423_init()` does it, the untouched default is the *phone* parameter set, and the header's own note says the setting "affects tilt detection".
  3. **Every hands-free state change is confirmed on two channels — haptic and visual — and never on audio.** The DRV2605L fires a distinct waveform per event (armed · take started · take stopped · take discarded · clip warning); the same event simultaneously changes a persistent on-screen state indicator that does not rely on colour alone. Waveforms must be distinguishable *without looking*, because the point of the confirmation is that the singer's eyes are on the score or the room. No confirmation is ever audible: the MAX98357A keeps its calibration-tone role ([ADR 0003](0003-microphone-path.md)) and nothing else.
  4. **Mode changes and arming are timed, not tapped.** A wrist raise brings the analyzer live; after a settle window the device auto-arms; a wrist drop auto-disarms after a hold-off. **Every one of those intervals is `TBD`** (decision D2 below) — they are Kconfig/preset values in the firmware, and this record deliberately does not invent them.
  5. **The display must be readable at arm's length in daylight.** Concretely: the mid-phrase readouts (f0 → note/cents, clipping flag, armed/recording state) occupy a fixed *glance zone* that never re-flows between presets; they are sized for the 5th–95th-percentile wrist-to-mouth reach rather than a round number ([03 #21](../bibliography/03-standards.md): ISO 7250-1 landmarks, ANSUR II / DINED distributions); state is never encoded by colour alone ([03 #23](../bibliography/03-standards.md), [09 S3](../bibliography/09-visual-feedback-for-singing.md)); and glance duration and touch-target sizing follow the small-screen guidance ([09 R3](../bibliography/09-visual-feedback-for-singing.md) watchOS HIG, [09 R4](../bibliography/09-visual-feedback-for-singing.md) Wear OS quality guidelines). The panel's actual luminance is **unmeasured** — the ≈ 450 cd/m² figure that appears both in the critique (as a small-screen sunlight-legibility rule of thumb) and in [09 §C](../bibliography/09-visual-feedback-for-singing.md) (as this panel's brightness) has **no source in either place** and stays `(prov.)` until an outdoor condition is run.
  6. **Accessibility is recorded here as a design constraint with teeth, not as a checkbox.** (a) No cue anywhere in the interaction model is audio-only. (b) No state is colour-only; every colour-coded state also has a shape, a position or a numeral ([03 #23](../bibliography/03-standards.md), [03 #24](../bibliography/03-standards.md); the CVD-safe colormap of [ADR 0011](0011-spectrogram-colormap.md) is the same argument applied to the spectrogram). (c) Every hands-free gesture has a touch equivalent that is reachable when the singer is not singing, so a user who cannot perform the gesture — limited shoulder range, prosthesis, seated arm position — is never locked out of a function. (d) The deaf/HoH singer is a first-class user of this device, which is why decision D3 may not end with "voice is the only way to start a take".
  7. **The DRV2605L driver is ours.** SensorLib 0.4.1 resolves `SensorDRV2605.hpp` but its 46 member definitions live in a `.cpp` outside `SRC_DIRS`, so any use is an undefined reference at link ([SensorLib notes](../reference-projects/notes/sensorlib_notes.md) §3). Of the three ways out — re-register the file locally, wait for the release that ships master's layout, or write ≈ 60 lines against `i2c_master` — the default taken here is **write our own**, using the DRV2605L ROM waveform library ([01 #25](../bibliography/01-datasheets.md)); it keeps `twatch_bsp` free of a per-release patch and does not put a second vendor tree on the link line. Revisit trigger: the SensorLib release that moves `haptic/` into `SRC_DIRS` with a Kconfig switch.
  8. **This ADR may not relax the sleep gate.** BMA423 INT1 is a *wake source*, never a sleep *trigger*. Nothing here authorises an unconditional `esp_light_sleep_start()`; the gate of [ADR 0015](0015-anti-brick-policy.md) item 5 (uptime ≥ N s **and** no USB host **and** the development arming flag **and** a timer wake) stands unchanged, and wrist-raise wake is developed with the sleep path off until a hardware experiment covers it.

- **What the author must decide** (this is the park; the firmware cannot be written past a stub until these four are answered):
  - **D1 — the gesture set.** Wrist raise alone, or wrist raise **plus** double-tap on the case (`BMA423_WAKEUP` `0x20`, single/double selectable, sensitivity tunable — [SensorLib notes](../reference-projects/notes/sensorlib_notes.md) §4.3) as an explicit start/stop, or **plus** the crown? Each extra gesture buys explicitness and costs a false-positive rate that has to be measured on a wrist, not reasoned about. The crown is the AXP2101 PWRKEY, not a GPIO, and a long press is the PMU power-off at 6 s ([ADR 0015](0015-anti-brick-policy.md)); any crown semantics must not collide with that.
  - **D2 — the timings.** Wrist-raise → analyzer live; settle window before auto-arm; how long the wrist must stay up before a take actually starts; auto-disarm hold-off after a wrist drop; haptic pulse length and inter-pulse gap. **All `TBD`.** None is derivable from a datasheet: they come from the objective-3 in-use sessions ([proposal §2 O3, §4.4](../proposal/01-super-spectral-proposal.md)). Note that this budget is *not* the ≤ 80 ms acoustic-to-photon budget and must not be conflated with it — the latency anchors ([05 #83–#85](../bibliography/05-papers.md)) bound perception of one's own voice, not the tolerance for an arming gesture, for which no anchor has been found.
  - **D3 — may a take be started by voice at all?** Three options, one already dead. **(a) Never** — a take starts only on a gesture or a timer; the boundary is deterministic and the record is trivially reproducible. **(b) Voice-onset auto-arm** — a level/onset detector reading the PCM ring that the analyzer already fills opens a take when phonation starts, with pre-roll taken from the ring so the attack is not lost. The detector **reads** the ring and never gates, ducks or alters it; the moment it touches the audio it violates the no-non-linear-pre-processing rule ([ADR 0003](0003-microphone-path.md), CLAUDE.md rule 8). Its cost is that the take boundary becomes a function of a threshold, which then has to appear in the take record and in every reported number. **(c) A spoken keyword** — already excluded: it needs ESP-SR, which CLAUDE.md rule 8 forbids outright, and it is useless to the deaf/HoH and non-speaking users decision 6 makes first-class. The author decides between (a) and (b). The accessibility argument cuts *toward* (b) — it is the only option that asks the user to do nothing at all, and it is the fallback for a singer who cannot raise the arm — and the metrology argument cuts *toward* (a).
  - **D4 — is "the screen is never touched during a take" an invariant or a default?** If an invariant, the state machine drops touch events outside `IDLE`/`REVIEW` and there is no setting. If a default, there is an escape hatch and the take record must carry a **touch-during-take flag**, in the same spirit as annotating a take with the BMA423 activity output (`STATIONARY`/`WALKING`/`RUNNING`) as a motion-artefact marker. Either way the record format is affected, so this is coordinated with [ADR 0010](0010-preset-schema.md) and [`../../protocols/specs/README.md`](../../protocols/specs/README.md).

- **Alternatives:**
  - *Touch-driven arming — a "record" button on the screen, as every phone recorder does it.* **Rejected.** It is precisely the input the use case forbids: it moves the microphone during the measurement, occludes the port, and costs the phrase. Revisit trigger: none — if this were acceptable the device would not need to be on a wrist.
  - *Hand-rolled wrist-raise from raw ±2 g / 50 Hz accelerometer samples.* **Rejected for now.** The Bosch feature engine's tilt is a tuned, platform-selected detector (phone vs wristband parameter sets) that we cannot reproduce from a datasheet, and the blob it needs is already on the link line for other reasons. **Revisit trigger:** the [ADR 0004](0004-split-licensing.md) licence review concluding that the 6 144-byte blob may not be redistributed inside our binaries ([SensorLib notes](../reference-projects/notes/sensorlib_notes.md) §4.2) — in which case this alternative is not optional, it is the whole ADR, rewritten with a measured false-positive rate for our own detector.
  - *A spoken keyword ("record").* **Rejected outright, no revisit trigger.** It requires ESP-SR (CLAUDE.md rule 8), its front end is exactly the non-linear pre-processing the analysis path forbids, and it excludes the users decision 6 names as first-class.
  - *Audible confirmation through the MAX98357A instead of haptics.* **Rejected.** An audio cue is invisible to the accessibility case, and the speaker shares an enclosure with the measurement microphone — a confirmation tone at the instant a take opens is a signal the analyzer would faithfully measure. Revisit trigger: none for confirmations; the amp keeps its calibration-tone role.
  - *A phone companion app as the remote control.* **Rejected** by [ADR 0017](0017-no-radio-in-v1-trimmed-component-set.md) — there is no radio in v1, and tenet 2 of [`../architecture/README.md`](../architecture/README.md) says the watch must be useful with no phone. **Revisit trigger:** the v2 radio decision, if one is ever taken.
  - *The crown (AXP2101 PWRKEY) as the arming button.* **Deferred, not rejected** — it is a one-finger, no-screen input that does not touch the glass, and it is the natural home for a deliberate "start now". It needs the PMU driver first (`twatch_pmu_axp2101.c`, roadmap E2) and a characterisation of press/hold behaviour that does not collide with the 6 s power-off. **Revisit trigger:** that driver landing with PWRKEY timing measured; it then feeds D1.
  - *Ship touch-only for v1 and add gestures later.* **Rejected.** The interaction model determines the take boundary, and the take boundary is in the record format; retrofitting it invalidates every take recorded before the change.

- **Consequences:**
  - (+) Nothing on the path of a take needs a finger, so the wrist-position envelope is measured on a wrist that is not being poked mid-take — the interaction model stops being a confound of the headline measurement.
  - (+) The accessibility framing is on the record as four enforceable rules (no audio-only cue, no colour-only state, a non-gesture path to every function, deaf/HoH as first-class) rather than as an intention; none of them costs anything today and none of them is cheap to retrofit.
  - (+) The BMA423 feature engine is already paid for — the blob is on the link line for [ADR 0018](0018-first-reference-project-study.md) reasons — so wrist-raise is configuration, not new DSP.
  - (−) **Every gesture has a false-positive and a false-negative rate, and neither is known.** A tilt detector tuned by Bosch for "wake the screen" optimises a different cost function than "open a scientific recording": a false arm wastes flash and pollutes the take set, a false negative loses a phrase that will not be sung the same way twice. Measuring it needs a wrist, and it is not yet on any schedule.
  - (−) Every timing in the model is `TBD` and stays `TBD` until the O3 sessions run. The firmware therefore ships tunables, and a tunable is a number nobody has defended.
  - (−) The BMA423 costs ≈ 350 ms of blocking init on core 0 before the DSP task exists (192 I²C transactions for the 6 144-byte blob plus a 150 ms busy-wait), and its running current with the feature engine enabled is **not yet in the power budget** — [01 #22](../bibliography/01-datasheets.md) is the source and has not been read for that figure. The ≥ 3 h autonomy bound is asserted against a budget with a hole in it.
  - (−) Haptics require BLDO2, which the rail policy keeps off until needed ([`../hw/twatch-s3-pins.md`](../hw/twatch-s3-pins.md)), so every confirmation is a rail transition plus the DRV2605L's own start-up time, which is unread. Enable-on-demand may not be fast enough for a confirmation that has to feel immediate; leaving the rail on costs quiescent current the budget has not accounted for either. This is a measurement, not a preference.
  - (−) **The haptic pulse is an acoustic and mechanical event the measurement microphone will record.** A confirmation buzz at the instant a take opens sits inside that take. The mitigation — fire confirmations strictly before the record window opens or after it closes, with a guard interval — is currently a design intention with an **unmeasured** guard interval.
  - (−) Hands-free is not universal. A singer who cannot raise the arm cannot arm a take by tilt; the touch fallback exists but is on the wrong side of the rule, and unless D3 lands on voice-onset there is no hands-free path for that user at all.
  - (−) "Readable at arm's length in daylight" is presently an aspiration, not a requirement with a number: the panel's luminance is unmeasured, the ≈ 450 cd/m² figure has no source, and the acoustic-path factorial ([proposal §4.4](../proposal/01-super-spectral-proposal.md)) varies SPL, distance and arm angle but has **no illuminance factor**. Adding one grows the matrix.
  - (−) We now own a DRV2605L driver we did not plan to write (decision 7), and a second C++ translation unit with a C-linkage facade in `twatch_bsp` for the BMA423 side.
  - Flagged as a validation item.
