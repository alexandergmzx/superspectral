# 0005 — No clinical claim: Super Spectral is a practice instrument for singers, and pathology corpora are acoustic material only

- **Status:** accepted
- **Amended 2026-08-21 (b):** the FDA guidance this record leans on was checked against its own text rather than a
  registry. It is **three revisions deep** — 2016-07-29 → 2019-09-27 → **2026-01-06** — and the current edition's cover
  page states the supersession explicitly. (A bibliography pass earlier the same day had concluded from the Federal
  Register API that no 2019 revision existed; FDA does not file an FR notice per revision, and that conclusion is
  retracted in [03 #34](../bibliography/03-standards.md).) **The constraint this record places on wording survives the
  revision unchanged** — §II's two factors and the §520(o)(1)(B) exclusion are quoted in the Context above — so no rule
  below moves. What changes is that the citation now names an edition, because "the FDA General Wellness guidance"
  without a date has meant three different documents since 2016.
- **Amended 2026-08-21:** the Saarbrücken licence was located and the corpus counts recomputed from the
  source. Licence: **CC BY 4.0** (Zenodo 16874898). Counts, from the site's own `voice_data.csv`:
  2 225 sessions, 1 853 speakers, 869 normal / 1 356 pathological *sessions*, 854 / 1 020 / 21-both
  speakers, **71 pathology labels in 186 combinations**. The figure this record previously carried —
  "1 356 patients" — was sessions counted as patients, and the "687 healthy" quoted alongside it in the
  bibliography matches nothing in the index. **Rule 4 is unchanged by any of this:** a permissive licence
  removes the redistribution question and touches none of the label question.
- **Date:** 2026-08-21
- **Context:** The feature set drifts into regulated territory silently if nobody writes the boundary down. The host path computes LTAS, spectral tilt, H1–H2 and CPP/CPPS — and AVQI is one line of arithmetic away from measures already being calculated ([05 #26–#30](../bibliography/05-papers.md), Maryn et al. 2010 = 05 #29). The validation plan feeds the analyzer with **Tier-3 pathology corpora**: PVQD ([10 #18](../bibliography/10-datasets-and-ground-truth.md), 296 WAVs with expert CAPE-V ratings, CC BY 4.0) and the Saarbrücken Voice Database ([10 #19](../bibliography/10-datasets-and-ground-truth.md), 1 356 *pathological sessions* from 1 020 speakers across 71 pathology labels, audio + EGG, **CC BY 4.0** — see the amendment below). The measurement protocols the §4 protocol section follows — ASHA/Patel et al. 2018 ([05 #31](../bibliography/05-papers.md)) and the ELS basic protocol / Dejonckere et al. 2001 ([05 #32](../bibliography/05-papers.md)) — are clinical documents. Under EU law the question is settled by **intended purpose**, not by the mathematics: MDR Article 2(1) qualifies a device by the purpose *the manufacturer assigns to it*, and Annex VIII Rule 11 ([03 #32](../bibliography/03-standards.md)) then classifies "software intended to provide information which is used to take decisions with diagnosis or therapeutic purposes" as class IIa or higher, with a second paragraph capturing software "intended to monitor physiological processes". MDCG 2019-11 ([03 #33](../bibliography/03-standards.md); plain-language map at [07 #22](../bibliography/07-technical-reports.md)) walks qualification *before* classification, so the whole Rule-11 branch is reached only if a medical purpose is claimed in the first place. The US side has a policy rather than a rule: the FDA *General Wellness: Policy for Low Risk Devices* ([03 #34](../bibliography/03-standards.md), **edition issued 2026-01-06** — read in full 2026-08-21, see the amendment) leaves a low-risk product outside device regulation only while its intended use makes **no reference to a disease or condition**. Verbatim, §II: CDRH "does not intend to examine low risk general wellness products" that meet two factors — *"(1) are intended for only general wellness use … and (2) present a low risk to the safety of users and other persons"* — and it names *"audio recordings, video games, software programs"* among the examples. The statutory hook is FD&C §520(o)(1)(B), which excludes software functions "intended for maintaining or encouraging a healthy lifestyle and … unrelated to the diagnosis, cure, mitigation, prevention, or treatment of a disease or condition". Separately, the same "not placed on the market" premise carries the radio question: the T-Watch S3 is a bare **chip-down ESP32-S3-R8, not a certified module**, so no FCC grant, RED declaration or Bluetooth SIG qualification is inherited ([03 #25](../bibliography/03-standards.md), [07 #21](../bibliography/07-technical-reports.md)).
- **Decision:** State the boundary once, and make every downstream sentence obey it.
  1. **Intended purpose (normative wording, quoted verbatim wherever one is needed).** *Super Spectral is a practice and measurement instrument for singers. It displays acoustic properties of the sound reaching its microphone. It does not detect, diagnose, screen for, monitor, prevent, treat or alleviate any disease or condition, and its output must not be used to take any such decision.* Under MDR Article 2(1) that is not a medical purpose, so the software is **not a medical device**; MDCG 2019-11's decision path stops at qualification and Rule 11 never engages. Under the FDA policy it is a general-wellness intended use of the first category (a general state of health / a healthy activity, with **no disease reference at all**) on a low-risk device — passive listening, no energy delivered to the body, no invasive part.
  2. **Wording rules, binding on UI labels, `README`s, the proposal, papers, abstracts, screenshots, preset names and commit messages.** Banned: *diagnosis / diagnostic, screening, patient, pathology detection, dysphonia (as an output), vocal health, voice health monitoring, hoarseness score, normal / abnormal voice, healthy vs unhealthy, vocal fatigue detection, damage, risk of injury, therapy / therapeutic, treatment, clinical-grade, medical-grade*. Used instead: *singer, user, practice, training, take, session, acoustic measure, spectral tilt, relative, within-session*. A measure keeps its acoustic definition in the label ("CPP — cepstral peak prominence, dB") and never acquires a clinical gloss.
  3. **No score, no threshold, no verdict.** No readout is normalised into a 0–100 index, a pass/fail, a normal/abnormal band, or a red/amber/green health semantic; no cross-session "trend" is presented as a health trajectory; no alert fires on any value. Timbre readouts stay relative, within-subject and within-session with a level co-report ([ADR 0008](README.md), proposal §7.1) — which is also the honest reading of the effect sizes, independent of regulation.
  4. **Pathology corpora are acoustic material.** Their audio may be injected on the digital-injection path; their **labels are never used as targets**. Concretely, and permanently: no healthy-vs-pathological classifier is trained or shipped; no sensitivity, specificity, ROC or AUC against pathology labels is computed or published; no per-file or per-speaker statement about a recorded voice is made anywhere; nothing beyond aggregate, corpus-level numbers leaves the analysis. PVQD's CAPE-V ratings may be used for one purpose only — a **construct-validity check** that an acoustic measure orders voices monotonically with expert perception, reported as a rank correlation over the corpus ([ADR 0002](0002-companion-architecture.md) compare mode) — and that result is stated as a property of the measure, never as detection performance. Every corpus entry carries `clinical_claim: "no"` — a required field with exactly one permitted value ([`datasets/README.md`](../../datasets/README.md), [`datasets/corpora/manifest.yaml`](../../datasets/corpora/manifest.yaml)); the corpora table in [`../validation/README.md`](../validation/README.md) already says "acoustic material … only" for Tier 3 and stays that way.
  5. **Clinical protocols are cited as measurement protocols.** ASHA (05 #31) and ELS (05 #32) fix mouth-to-mic distance, microphone class, SPL calibration and analysis settings; those are the only things taken from them. AVQI and CPPS remain computable on the host as acoustic measures with their published definitions — printed with no cut-off, no severity band and no interpretation.
  6. **Sessions with singers are usability and measurement sessions, not clinical investigations.** Participants are recruited as singers, never for a voice complaint; nothing is told to a participant about their voice health; recruitment, consent and debrief copy obey rule 2; a health condition a participant volunteers is not recorded in any take, manifest or note.
  7. **Not placed on the market — the premise both boundaries rest on.** The project is a research prototype: one bench unit (`48:27:e2:e9:b0:8c`, [`../hw/README.md`](../hw/README.md)), not supplied to third parties, not offered for distribution. Publishing the repository under Apache-2.0 ([ADR 0004](0004-split-licensing.md)) places *source* in the world, not a device — but the `README` travels with the code, so rules 1–3 bind the repository exactly as strictly as they bind the watch UI. On the radio side nothing is inherited from a module ([03 #25](../bibliography/03-standards.md), [07 #21](../bibliography/07-technical-reports.md)) and nothing is operated: [ADR 0017](0017-no-radio-in-v1-trimmed-component-set.md) removed `esp_wifi`, `bt`, `lwip` and `esp_phy` from the build entirely (verified 2026-08-21 — the symbols do not exist in `sdkconfig`) and the SX1262 stays in reset with ALDO4 off. Were a radio ever enabled on a unit placed on the EU market, EN 300 328 ([03 #26](../bibliography/03-standards.md)), EN 301 489-1/-17 ([03 #27](../bibliography/03-standards.md)) and the RF-exposure assessment for a body-worn device ([03 #28](../bibliography/03-standards.md)) would be owed with it, per unit and per case revision.
  8. **Enforcement, and where it is weak.** Today the rules are procedural: a wording pass over the proposal §7.1 bullet, the root [`README`](../../README.md), [`host/README.md`](../../host/README.md) and each abstract before anything is published. A `no-clinical-claim` grep hook (banned-term list of rule 2, over `ui` string tables and `docs/**`) joins [`.pre-commit-config.yaml`](../../.pre-commit-config.yaml) when the [`ui`](../../firmware/twatch-s3/components/ui/README.md) component gains its first user-visible string table in Phase 1; the component has none yet, which is why the hook does not exist yet. Any feature that infers emotion, confidence or affect re-opens this ADR before it is written ([03 #35](../bibliography/03-standards.md), AI Act Article 5(1)(f)).
- **Alternatives:**
  - *Make a "vocal health" claim and shelter under the FDA general-wellness policy.* Rejected: the policy is US-only and has no EU counterpart, and a health-monitoring label is exactly what Rule 11's second paragraph captures. One label would reclassify the whole project.
  - *Avoid pathology corpora entirely and stay clean by abstinence.* Rejected: PVQD and Saarbrücken are the only perceptually rated and EGG-paired material for the H1–H2 / CPP axis (10 #18, #19), and the boundary is enforceable by rules on *use*. Revisit trigger (**did not fire**, 2026-08-21 — see the amendment): if the Saarbrücken site terms turn out to forbid non-clinical secondary use, the corpus drops out on licence grounds, not on this one.
  - *Pursue genuine MDR Class IIa conformity.* Rejected for this project: QMS, technical documentation, clinical evaluation and a notified body are a multi-year programme orthogonal to the research question. Revisit trigger: a decision to build a product for speech-language pathologists — which would be a new ADR, not an amendment to this one.
  - *A disclaimer in the root `README` and nothing else.* Rejected: intended purpose is read from **all** the material a manufacturer puts out (labels, screenshots, paper titles), per MDCG 2019-11; a disclaimer under a screenshot that says "dysphonia" is worth nothing.
  - *Say nothing and decide later.* Rejected: "later" is after the wording has already spread through a thesis, a repository and a UI.
- **Consequences:**
  - (+) The qualification question is answered once, in one file, in the regulator's own vocabulary; §7.1, the dataset ledger, the validation corpora table and the bibliography all point here instead of re-arguing it.
  - (+) Tier-3 corpora stay usable — the acoustic value of 1 356 pathological *sessions* with EGG is kept while the diagnostic reading is refused.
  - (−) Genuinely interesting analyses are permanently off the table: a CPPS-vs-Saarbrücken-labels ROC is the obvious paper and this ADR forbids it. That cost is accepted, not hedged.
  - (−) Every user-visible string, screenshot, abstract and consent form now needs a wording review, forever — and until the Phase-1 grep hook exists, the review is a habit rather than a gate.
  - (−) The boundary is asserted by us and certified by nobody. A regulator reading a future feature could disagree; the only mitigation is to re-open this ADR *before* such a feature is written, which relies on the author noticing.
  - (−) The "not placed on the market" premise carries both the MDR and the RED conclusion. Handing a second unit to a collaborator to keep, or enabling a radio, invalidates half of this record at once.
  - (−) ~~The Saarbrücken terms are unverified~~ — **resolved 2026-08-21.** The database is deposited on Zenodo under **CC BY 4.0** ([record 16874898](https://zenodo.org/records/16874898), DOI 10.5281/zenodo.16874898, creators Pützer & Barry). A licence *could* have tightened rule 4; this one does not — CC BY grants use and redistribution against attribution, which is weaker than the constraint this record already imposes. The site itself has no terms page at all: its "Terms & Conditions" footer heading covers an Impressum and a privacy link, and `/terms`, `/nutzungsbedingungen` and the legacy `/help_en.php4` are 404. What remains open is **not** a licence question but roadmap **Q41** — whether a clinician-labelled pathology corpus feeds a headline metric at all — and that is the owner's ethics call, not a lookup ([`../roadmap/documentation-roadmap.md`](../roadmap/documentation-roadmap.md)).
