# 0018 — What the reference projects supply is a specification, not code: per-component takeaways from xiao-edge-audio, LilyGoLib/XPowersLib, esp-dsp and SensorLib

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-21
- **Context:** [`docs/reference-projects/README.md`](../reference-projects/README.md) states the policy inherited from `swarm` — *"catalogue here, clone on demand"*: read the code upstream or in a shallow clone, capture takeaways in a note, then lift the conclusions into an ADR, and name **0018** as the first project-study ADR. The policy was written in roadmap D3 and **never exercised**. Until 2026-08-21 no clone had been read end-to-end, no note existed, and every "what to take" cell in [bibliography 06](../bibliography/06-reference-projects.md) was an *intention* — plausible, uncited, and in three cases wrong. Meanwhile the components those cells describe ([`audio_source`](../../firmware/twatch-s3/components/audio_source/README.md), [`spectral_fft_backend`](../../firmware/twatch-s3/components/spectral_fft_backend/README.md), [`twatch_bsp`](../../firmware/twatch-s3/components/twatch_bsp/README.md), [`ui`](../../firmware/twatch-s3/components/ui/README.md)) already carried decisions leaning on them, and [ADR 0001](0001-toolchain-esp-idf-v6-pinned-environment.md) had pinned two of the projects as dependencies. The loop was run on 2026-08-21 over four studies, tracked as durable notes with a studied commit and a licence read from each project's own `LICENSE` ([`docs/reference-projects/notes/`](../reference-projects/notes/README.md)): [xiao-edge-audio](../reference-projects/notes/xiao-edge-audio_notes.md) `3b8de19` (MIT), [esp-dsp](../reference-projects/notes/esp-dsp_notes.md) `3c8ac0f` (Apache-2.0 — the master merge of the v1.8.2 change; **corrected 2026-08-21**, `git checkout v1.8.2` lands on `a53a0756`, and the two commits share tree `f68540c0`, so the bytes read are the tag's but the sha is not. The same equation is still written in [`esp-dsp_notes.md`](../reference-projects/notes/esp-dsp_notes.md) §Reference basis and is owed the same correction), [SensorLib](../reference-projects/notes/sensorlib_notes.md) `2b9e591` + registry `0.4.1` `eb4217bd…c27d3` (MIT), [LilyGoLib + XPowersLib](../reference-projects/notes/lilygolib-axp2101_notes.md) `38e6f8d` / `d699758` (both MIT). This ADR is the loop's output. Where a study and a measurement of 2026-08-20/21 disagree, the measurement wins ([`docs/hw/README.md`](../hw/README.md), [ADR 0016](0016-backlight-gpio45-vdd-spi-strap.md), [ADR 0017](0017-no-radio-in-v1-trimmed-component-set.md), [experiment 0002](../validation/experiments/0002-rollback-and-boot-guard-race.md)).
- **Decision:**

  **0. The transfer rule.** Nothing is copied. Three things cross the boundary: **hardware facts** (register addresses, bit positions, voltage encodings, pin roles — facts about copper and silicon, not expression), **call sequences and argument lists** already validated on this silicon class, and **defect knowledge**. The firmware link line admits exactly what [`dependencies.lock`](../../firmware/twatch-s3/dependencies.lock) pins, and **of the four projects studied here only two are on it** — `espressif/esp-dsp ~1.8.2` and `lewisxhe/sensorlib ~0.4.1`. (**Corrected 2026-08-21:** this sentence read as though the lock pinned only those two. It pins **eight** components — esp-dsp 1.8.2, esp_codec_dev 1.6.2, esp_lcd_touch 1.2.1, esp_lcd_touch_ft5x06 1.1.1, esp_lvgl_port 2.9.0, littlefs 1.22.3, sensorlib 0.4.1, lvgl 9.5.0 — plus `idf 6.0.2`; the other six are simply not reference-project studies.) LilyGoLib, XPowersLib, TTGO_TWatch_Library and xiao-edge-audio are **never added as components**, although all four are MIT and could be; they are read-only sources of the sequences above. Every note names the commit it read, so each claim is re-checkable at a version bump ([`upgrade-procedure.md`](../devenv/upgrade-procedure.md)).

  **1. `audio_source` takes xiao-edge-audio's driver sequence and rejects its framing.**
  - **Take** (≈40 lines): `I2S_CHANNEL_DEFAULT_CONFIG(I2S_NUM_0, I2S_ROLE_MASTER)` → `i2s_new_channel(…, NULL, &rx)` → `i2s_pdm_rx_config_t` → `i2s_channel_init_pdm_rx_mode()` → `i2s_channel_enable()` → `i2s_channel_read()`, changing pins (CLK 44 / DATA 47), rate (32 kHz, `DSR_8S` ⇒ 2.048 MHz), and the slot macro (the explicit PCM-format one; the v5 alias survives on v6.0.2 only as a `@cond`). `dma_desc_num`/`dma_frame_num` are the ring-depth pair and are sized in **milliseconds of hop slack** with the arithmetic written next to them (mono 16-bit ⇒ 2 B/frame; xiao's 6 × 240 = 2 880 B = 90 ms, which its own comment calls "~30 ms"). `int16 → float × 1/32768` at the seam. On v6.0.2 the dependency is `esp_driver_i2s`, not the legacy `driver` umbrella.
  - **Reject — block-synchronous framing (hop = N).** It caps rows/s at `fs/N` (15.6 at xiao's defaults; its README's "20 fps" is a rounding artefact of a bursty 500 ms counter) and sets latency ≥ `N/fs`. Ours decouples them: a PCM ring in `MALLOC_CAP_INTERNAL | MALLOC_CAP_DMA`, a hop counter firing an analysis frame every `hop` samples over the last `N` — 32 kHz, `N = 4096` (128 ms, 7.8 Hz bins), hop 640 (20 ms) ⇒ **50 rows/s at 84 % overlap**. The same ring is where the digital-injection path writes corpus WAV (two-path rule).
  - **Reject — `portMAX_DELAY` reads and silent DMA overruns.** Real timeouts, and an `on_recv_q_ovf` callback incrementing a dropout counter. **Two independent precedents make the same mistake**: xiao's blocking read, and LilyGoLib's `PDM::recordWAV()` single blocking `i2s_read()` of a whole take. A hung I²S must become a task-watchdog panic ([ADR 0015](0015-anti-brick-policy.md) item 6), never a quiet freeze.
  - **Reject — network or render I/O inside the DSP loop.** Harmless for xiao at 15 frames/s over Wi-Fi; fatal at 50 Hz over SPI. The analysis task hands off a finished frame and returns to the ring.
  - **What LilyGoLib settles is the constraint, not the value.** PDM RX is I2S0-only and 16-bit — asserted twice in its own comments, independently of the `ESP_RETURN_ON_FALSE` guards ADR 0003 cites. Its "16 kHz maximum" is a **legacy-driver artefact** traceable to one `#define`, with no datasheet basis (SPM1423 clock window 1.0–3.25 MHz), and its `use_apll = true` cannot mean anything on an S3. **Open question H-slot stays open**: the vendor answers it three incompatible ways (`ONLY_RIGHT`, `SLOT_LEFT`, and a factory example that de-interleaves a mono stream as stereo). It remains an E2 bench item.

  **2. `spectral_fft_backend` uses esp-dsp for the kernels and owns its memory, its windows and its dB axis.**
  - **There is no `fft4real` API** — `examples/fft4real/` is a directory, not a symbol. The real path is `fft2r`/`fft4r` + `dsps_bit_rev2r_fc32`/`dsps_bit_rev4r_fc32` + `dsps_cplx2real_fc32`, and **`dsps_cplx2real_fc32` requires `dsps_fft4r_init_fc32` even on the radix-2 path**. This corrects [bibliography 06 #1](../bibliography/06-reference-projects.md), the component README and the `main/idf_component.yml` comment.
  - **Radix per preset** *(**amended 2026-08-21** — [ADR 0006](0006-fft-normalisation-and-window-conventions.md) D6 supersedes the recommendation, not the fact: v1 uses **`fft2r` for every size**, because carrying two kernels buys a faster path for two of six presets at the cost of a second code path, a second set of esp-dsp globals and a second alignment rule. Revisit on an H13 on-target cycle count. The observation below stands and is why the split was tempting)*: `N_c = N_real/2`, radix-4 needs `N_c = 4^k`, so fft4r would serve real-2048 and real-8192; fft2r serves real-1024 and **real-4096 — the default analysis size, which cannot use the radix-4 kernel.**
  - **Always pass our own buffers.** esp-dsp's internal allocations are not capability-aware (`malloc`/`memalign` → the default heap), and with our `CONFIG_SPIRAM_MALLOC_ALWAYSINTERNAL=16384` the fft4r twiddle table (32 KB at `N_c = 2048`, 64 KB at 4096) lands **in PSRAM** — an architecture-tenet-3 violation on every butterfly. Both inits therefore receive `heap_caps_aligned_alloc(16, …, MALLOC_CAP_INTERNAL | MALLOC_CAP_8BIT)`, never `NULL`. Init **once at boot with the largest `N_c` any preset uses**: a second init is silently ignored and returns `ESP_OK`, and a table smaller than `N` is a silent over-read with no error (verified against a standalone build).
  - **Alignment is required and never checked.** The `_aes3` FFT kernels use `ee.ldf.64`/`ee.stf.64` (8 B; esp-dsp's own examples use 16 B) and a misaligned base is a `LoadStoreAlignment` panic, not an error code. 16-byte alignment plus an assert at init on every buffer handed to the library.
  - **Unpack slot 0.** `dsps_cplx2real_fc32` packs `X[0]` in `slot[0].re` and `X[N_real/2]` in `slot[0].im`; `sqrt(re²+im²)` on slot 0 mixes DC with Nyquist (esp-dsp's own example computes exactly that wrong value). We emit `N/2 + 1` bins. The transform is the unnormalised forward DFT — DC and Nyquist scale with `N`, all other bins with `N/2` — so ADR 0006's one-sided ×2 applies to bins `1 … N/2−1` **only**.
  - **Generate the windows ourselves.** All six `dsps_wind_*_f32` use `1/(len−1)` — **symmetric**, where ADR 0006 mandates periodic — and evaluate a `double` `M_PI` product per sample per term, which is exactly what our `-Wdouble-promotion` flag exists to catch. The **coefficient sets are correct**, but this record named one of them wrongly (**corrected 2026-08-21**, against Heinzel 2002 Table 3 read from the report, [05 #1](../bibliography/05-papers.md)): `blackman_harris` = Harris's 4-term −92 dB set (0.35875 / 0.48829 / 0.14128 / 0.01168) as stated; but esp-dsp's **`nuttall`** (0.355768 / 0.487396 / 0.144232 / 0.012604) is Heinzel's **Nuttall4b** — sidelobe decay f^−3, PSLL −93.3 dB, NENBW 2.0212 bins — **not** the minimum-4-term. The minimum-4-term is Heinzel's **Nuttall4c** (0.3635819 / 0.4891775 / 0.1365995 / 0.0106411, PSLL −98.1 dB, NENBW 1.9761 bins), which esp-dsp ships under the name **`blackman_nuttall`** and which is what SciPy's `nuttall` returns. Our own committed constants already agree with Heinzel to four decimals ([`preset-schema.md`](../../protocols/specs/preset-schema.md) §4.3, which carries the same correction), and [ADR 0006](0006-fft-normalisation-and-window-conventions.md) D1 records the resulting name trap: a host lane that resolves the string `nuttall` through SciPy compares two different windows, at `max|Δ| = 0.0163` — a silent bias, not a rounding residue. So the denominator was never the only thing at issue — the *name* was; our periodic tables are checksummed into [`golden-files.md`](../validation/golden-files.md).
  - **`fc32` only, no `sc16`** — confirmed with arithmetic, not preference: the fixed butterfly shifts right by 16, i.e. **1/2 per stage, 1/N overall, `log2 N` bits lost** (12 at `N_c = 4096`, ≈4 bits left for a low-level bin), with no exponent tracking. It buys ≈6× for a display range we need. `dsps_snr_f32`/`dsps_sfdr_f32` stay out of firmware entirely — the first claims the global FFT table behind our back.
  - **Corrections to our own numbers.** `CONFIG_DSP_MAX_FFT_SIZE` bounds `N_c`, **not** `N_real`: the default 4096 already covers a real-8192 FFT on the real path — **no `sdkconfig.defaults` change is needed** (this retracts the xiao note's §3 claim). And real-8192 costs **≈160 KB** with esp-dsp's own tables, **≈104 KB** if we write our own `cplx2real` twiddles (`4·(N_c+2)` bytes instead of 65 536); the 112 KB figure in [02 #21](../bibliography/02-application-notes.md) holds only in the second case, which is therefore what the backend does.
  - **One owner for esp-dsp's globals** (`dsps_fft_w_table_fc32`, `dsps_fft4r_w_table_fc32`, the `*_initialized` flags): `spectral_fft_backend`. One init, one deinit; host and QEMU tests may not call anything that inits behind our back.
  - **Two upstream defects** ([ADR 0017](0017-no-radio-in-v1-trimmed-component-set.md) already promised the report): `va_start` without `va_end` in `aes3_tie_log.c`, and unchecked allocations in seven `dsps_*_init` functions — plus a use-before-assign in `dsps_firmr_init_s16` found by reading. The issue text is drafted in the note; `-fno-analyzer` for registry components stands until it lands.

  **3. `twatch_bsp` writes its own AXP2101 driver and takes the BMA423 through SensorLib.**
  - **(a) AXP2101 — hand-written, and no longer by preference.** `lewisxhe/sensorlib 0.4.1` — the artefact `~0.4.1` resolves to and the E1 gate built — **contains no AXP2101 code at all**; the X-Powers drivers exist only on GitHub master. The "schedule-pressure fallback" recorded in [06 #7](../bibliography/06-reference-projects.md), in ADR 0001's consequences and in the `twatch_bsp` README does not exist and must be **amended, not superseded**. XPowersLib remains a read-only register reference: it has no `idf_component.yml` and is not registry-published, so it cannot be pinned or locked (ADR 0001). Our ~300-line C99 driver over `i2c_master_bus_handle_t` is written from the note's register table, whose mandatory content is: chip ID `0x4A` at `0x03`; **`disableTSPinMeasure()`** (`0x50 ← (old & 0xF0) | 0x10`, `0x30` b1 ← 0) because this board has no NTC and leaving TS measurement on causes abnormal charging; voltages before enables, with LDO codes `(mV − 500)/100` in bits 4:0; **never write `0x80` bit 0 (DC1 = the SoC's own rail)**; and IRQ discipline mask-all → enable-wanted → clear-all → attach the pin last, with `INTSTS1` as the **low** byte of `getIrqStatus()` and a **level-low** handler (the IRQ pin is open-drain and stays low until the status registers are cleared, so an edge handler that misses a clear wedges silently).
  - **Our rail order stays ours.** LilyGoLib enables **ALDO2 (backlight) before ALDO3 (display+touch)** with no settle delay anywhere in `initPMU()`; ours is ALDO3 → ≥10 ms → ALDO2 last, and the gate implements it. Nothing in firmware changes — the **provenance line** in [`twatch-s3-pins.md`](../hw/twatch-s3-pins.md) ("Source: LilyGoLib `initPMU()` sequence") is what is wrong. Likewise "unused rails explicitly disabled at boot" is **our** policy: LilyGoLib leaves DC3, DC4 and BLDO1 enabled on every board, for a GPS the T-Watch-S3 does not have.
  - **The charger must be programmed deliberately.** The vendor writes a **4.35 V CV target** (`0x64 ← 4`) because this is a high-voltage cell; the POR default is 4.2 V, so a driver that never writes `0x64` silently under-charges by ≈5 % — directly on the ≥3 h autonomy metric. CC is 125 mA (`0x62 ← 5`), under the vendor's own <130 mA cap. The power-key off time is 4 s in LilyGoLib (`0x27`) against a 6 s POR default, so we write it explicitly and state the value — ADR 0015's consequence bullet currently says 6 s.
  - **Do not write gauge data.** `calibrationPMU()` streams a 128-byte blob into the PMU's gauge ROM — a one-way write to a peripheral we cannot re-image, whose output is a convenience readout, not a measurement instrument (autonomy is anchored to PPK2/Otii). ADR-gated if ever wanted.
  - **[ADR 0015](0015-anti-brick-policy.md) §10 is amended: the decision stands, the premise was wrong.** Neither XPowersLib's nor SensorLib's `init()` arms a PMU watchdog, and LilyGoLib never touches `0x18` b0 or `0x19`. The AXP2101 powers up with the watchdog **disabled** (`0x18` b0 = 0), a **64 s** timeout (`0x19[2:0]` = `110b`) and action **"IRQ only"** (`0x19[5:4]` = `00`). The "4 s watchdog armed by XPowersLib's init" came from XPowersLib's ESP-IDF *example* for a different board — with its `setWatchdogConfig()` line commented out, so even there it would only raise an IRQ. We leave all three at their defaults, for the reason ADR 0015 already gives.
  - **(b) BMA423 through SensorLib — the only reason SensorLib is on the link line.** The 6 144-byte Bosch feature-config blob (`.rodata.bma423_config_file`, confirmed in the compiled object) is not derivable from any datasheet, and without it the tilt engine ADR 0012 depends on does not exist. Consequences that become `twatch_bsp` rules: **`CONFIG_SENSORLIB_ESP_IDF_NEW_API=y` is an asserted invariant**, not a Kconfig default we happen to inherit — on v6.0.2 the legacy path still *compiles* and would install a second, EOL I²C driver on a bus `twatch_bsp` owns (this sharpens [pitfall B14](../devenv/pitfalls.md)); the caller is a **C++ TU with a C-linkage facade**; SensorLib's include directories are re-added with **`-isystem`** (its headers produce **73 `-Werror` diagnostics** under our strict set, and blanket-disabling `-Wdouble-promotion` is not acceptable on a single-precision FPU); the BMA423 is constructed through the **custom-HAL** path with our own `vTaskDelay`-backed `delay`, because `HalEspIDF::delay()` is `ms % portTICK_PERIOD_MS` — **an unconditional no-op at our `FREERTOS_HZ=1000`**, so the chip would get 0 ms instead of 20 ms after soft reset; and the whole init (192 I²C transactions for the blob plus a 150 ms **busy**-wait ⇒ ≈350 ms blocking) runs **on core 0 before the DSP task exists**. For ADR 0012, the single highest-value line is `selectPlatform(Platform::WRISTBAND)`: it selects a different 25-parameter set and **changes tilt behaviour**, and nothing in `bma423_init()` does it — the untouched default is the phone set.
  - **(c) A licence hole that must not be lost.** Of the 17 files in `src/bosch/bma4xx/`, 15 carry a 2023 Bosch copyright with explicit **BSD-3-Clause**; **`bma423.c` and `bma423.h` — the two that carry the blob and the BMA423 API — carry the legacy 2017–2018 Bosch Sensortec "Disclaimer/Special" notice: no SPDX, no BSD grant, and no redistribution permission of any kind.** Our *use* is squarely inside that notice's stated purpose (driving a genuine Bosch part), but [`NOTICE`](../../NOTICE) records the Bosch part as flatly "BSD-3-Clause" and is wrong for those two files, and SensorLib's own `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` cannot serve as our audit source — the SBOM step reads **per-file headers**. Two follow-ups are owed: verify upstream `boschsensortec/BMA423-Sensor-API` ([01 #23](../bibliography/01-datasheets.md), [06 #50](../bibliography/06-reference-projects.md)) and cite it if it is BSD-3-Clause; otherwise ADR 0004 names an explicit exception for a vendor feature blob used on the vendor's own part, or ADR 0012 drops the feature engine for wrist-raise computed from raw ±2 g / 50 Hz samples.
  - **(d) Touch: take register numbers, not a driver.** `esp_lcd_touch_ft5x06 ~1.1.1` stays the driver (it is what `lvgl_port_add_touch()` can consume), but it performs **no chip-ID check** — the property that lets it drive an FT6336U at all, and the one that makes a dead controller look like "no touches". `twatch_bsp` therefore reads `0xA8` (vendor ID), `0xA3` (chip ID, expect **`0x64`**) and `0xA6` (firmware version) once during the I²C gate and asserts. Those are documented register numbers, so no SensorLib touch code reaches the link line. `PMODE_DEEP_SLEEP` is never used: waking from it needs the reset pin, which is unpopulated on this board.
  - **(e) DRV2605L is not decided here.** SensorLib 0.4.1 does not compile `haptic_drivers/HapticDriver_DRV2605.cpp` (it is outside `SRC_DIRS`), so any use is an undefined reference at link. ADR 0012 must choose explicitly between waiting for a release, re-registering that file locally, and a ~60-line driver of our own.

  **4. `ui` and `display_backend` take the argument list and the geometry pairing, and nothing else.**
  - **Take:** the vendor's `esp_lcd` argument list — **brace-initialised**, because LilyGoLib leaves `flags` and `vendor_config` uninitialised and `esp_lcd_new_panel_st7789()` reads them from stack garbage; its ST7789 command list as a **starting point to bisect against** the in-tree driver's own init; and the **`(mirror_y, y_gap)` pairing** — `mirror_y = true ⇒ y_gap = 80`, `false ⇒ 0` — for the 240×240 window inside a 240×320 GRAM. One without the other is the classic 80-pixel garbage band.
  - **Orientation is a deliberate choice, not an inheritance.** Our gate ran `set_gap(0,0)` with no mirroring — LilyGoLib rotation **2**, i.e. 180° from the vendor's shipped rotation 0. `twatch_display.c` picks against the crown position, and [ADR 0007](README.md)'s scroll-axis check must be performed **in the final orientation**, because `VSCRDEF`/`VSCSAD` address the same 320-line GRAM the gap compensates for.
  - **Two timing facts to test, not to assume:** the vendor ships **80 MHz** pclk on `SPI3_HOST` where our gate proved 20 MHz on `SPI2_HOST` (so 80 MHz is achievable evidence, not a measurement of ours — bench stepping stays an E2 item), and `0xC6` FRCTRL2 caps the panel's own refresh and must be mapped against the ST7789V3 table ([01 #13](../bibliography/01-datasheets.md)) before ADR 0007's 50 Hz budget leans on anything.
  - **Reject:** the raw `0x36` MADCTL write behind `esp_lcd`'s back (it desynchronises the driver's cached state); the `setBrightness(0) ⇒ SLPIN` coupling (a backlight API that silently sleeps the panel is a trap for a redraw loop); the 2×-over-allocated clear buffer. The backlight ramp is a **LEDC duty** ramp, not a rail ramp — LilyGoLib's `incrementalBrightness(250, 20)` is ≈5 s of duty stepping — and the pins-doc wording must say so.
  - **Reject the spectrogram rendering of both demos:** xiao's linear-frequency axis with a hand-rolled 4-stop RGB gradient, and the vendor factory example's `(dB+40)/40` scale. Neither is perceptually uniform, RGB565-quantised or dithered (ADR 0011). More generally, **neither project has a calibrated dB axis**: xiao applies no `S1` normalisation, no one-sided ×2 and clamps at 0 dB, so a −48 dBFS sine already reads full-scale under its "dB FS" label. ADR 0006 owns that axis for both halves of the project.

- **Alternatives:**
  - *Port xiao-edge-audio file-by-file as the template.* **Rejected.** Its framing, window, dB scale and task coupling are each wrong for a 50 Hz, calibrated, cents-grade instrument; the driver sequence is the only part worth keeping and it is ≈40 lines. No revisit trigger — this is a judgement about our requirements, not about the project's quality as a demo.
  - *Vendor LilyGoLib / XPowersLib / TTGO into the tree (all MIT, all permitted).* **Rejected.** They are Arduino libraries whose pin macros are not even theirs — every `DISP_*`, `MIC_*`, `I2S_*` symbol resolves in **LGPL-2.1 `arduino-esp32/variants/`**, which was deliberately not opened. What we need is register sequences, which transfer as prose. No trigger.
  - *Use SensorLib's `PmicAXP2101` as the AXP2101 fallback, as ADR 0001 and 06 #7 recorded.* **Rejected: it does not exist at the pin.** Revisit trigger: a registry release ≥ 0.5.0 carrying master's refactor, evaluated through the upgrade procedure — and even then the hand-written driver likely stays, because our rail order, our disable-unused policy and our IRQ handling all differ from the vendor's.
  - *Track SensorLib's GitHub master (git dependency or vendored) to get the AXP2101 driver and the `CONFIG_SENSORLIB_EXCLUDE_*` matrix now.* **Rejected.** ADR 0001 forbids `path:`/git dependencies because they make `dependencies.lock` environment-specific, and master is unreleased, unversioned work. Same trigger as above. Until then, do **not** write `CONFIG_SENSORLIB_EXCLUDE_*` lines: the symbols do not exist at 0.4.1, `kconfgen` will not complain, and the lines would look effective while doing nothing.
  - *Drop SensorLib and hand-roll a BMA423 driver.* **Rejected** — the 6 144-byte feature blob is not derivable from any datasheet. Revisit trigger: the licence review concluding the blob may not be redistributed in our binaries, in which case the alternative is not a hand-rolled feature engine but wrist-raise computed from raw ±2 g / 50 Hz samples on the SoC — a different and cheaper-to-license ADR 0012.
  - *Vendor `boschsensortec/BMA423-Sensor-API` directly and skip SensorLib.* **Rejected for now, kept as the licence escape hatch.** It removes a C++ layer we barely use and gives a first-party licence header to cite, at the cost of an ≈80-line bus/delay shim and the loss of PCF8563/DRV2605 coverage. Revisit trigger: upstream's current `bma423.c` carrying the 2023/2024 BSD-3-Clause header while SensorLib's copy does not.
  - *Use SensorLib for touch and drop `esp_lcd_touch_ft5x06`.* **Rejected.** SensorLib's FT6X36 has the better identity gate but exposes no `esp_lcd_touch` handle, so `esp_lvgl_port` could not consume it and we would own the LVGL input plumbing. We take the register numbers instead. No trigger.
  - *Write our own FFT and skip esp-dsp.* **Rejected.** The `_aes3` PIE kernels are why the transform is a few percent of one core; ANSI C at real-4096 / 50 Hz is not. Writing our own `cplx2real` twiddle table (56 KB saved at real-8192) is a scoped exception inside the library's contract, not a fork. No trigger for the transform itself.
  - *Adopt `sc16` for speed.* **Rejected** with the arithmetic above. Revisit trigger: a block-floating-point layer, which esp-dsp does not provide and which would itself need validating against the float path — i.e. a new ADR, not a flag.
  - *Defer the study until the firmware actually needs each library.* **Rejected**, and this ADR is the evidence: three of the four studies corrected documents that had already been written and one of them (the missing AXP2101 driver) removed a fallback the toolchain ADR was counting on. Reading first is cheaper than discovering at integration.

- **Consequences:**
  - (+) Four components now have a written, commit-keyed specification where they had an intention: the PDM call sequence, the esp-dsp real-input sequence with its four corrections, the AXP2101 register table, the BMA423 init order and the ST7789 argument list. `twatch_bsp`'s PMU driver can be written without a clone open.
  - (+) Every "what to take" claim in [bibliography 06](../bibliography/06-reference-projects.md) for these four projects is now confirmed or corrected against a named commit, and the licence column is confirmed from each project's own `LICENSE` rather than from a catalogue cell.
  - (+) Cheap defect knowledge, bought by reading: the PSRAM twiddle-table trap, the silent no-op FFT re-init, the unchecked alignment, the packed DC/Nyquist slot, SensorLib's no-op `delay()` and infinite I²C timeouts, LilyGoLib's uninitialised `panel_config` and hidden `SLPIN`. Each of these would have cost hours at integration; several are invisible in testing (wrong output, no error).
  - (−) **Corrections are owed across the tree, and this ADR names them without performing them:** [`NOTICE`](../../NOTICE) (Bosch per-file licence), [`twatch-s3-pins.md`](../hw/twatch-s3-pins.md) (rail-order provenance, "explicitly disabled" attribution, backlight-ramp wording, 4.35 V CV target), ADR 0001's consequences and the `twatch_bsp` README (no SensorLib PMU fallback), ADR 0015 §10 and its 6 s power-off bullet, [06 #1](../bibliography/06-reference-projects.md) / [06 #3](../bibliography/06-reference-projects.md) / [06 #7](../bibliography/06-reference-projects.md), [bibliography 11](../bibliography/11-esp-idf-platform-and-toolchain.md), [pitfalls](../devenv/pitfalls.md) B14 and H4, the `spectral_fft_backend` README, the `main/idf_component.yml` comment, and the gate README's eFuse-default parenthetical. Until they land, two documents disagree in the tree.
  - (−) The hand-written AXP2101 driver is now **the only plan**, with no library fallback, on the part that gates every rail. If it is wrong, nothing powers up.
  - (−) SensorLib enters the link line with three defects we work around rather than fix (no-op `delay`, wait-forever I²C, a hard-coded 400 kHz for every device on the shared bus), 52 objects and a 9.0 MB archive that **cannot be trimmed at 0.4.1** (`--gc-sections` keeps the flash cost near zero; the cost is build time and review surface), and a `REQUIRES driver` on the legacy umbrella — shared with `esp_lcd_touch` and `esp_codec_dev`, so dropping SensorLib would not remove it ([ADR 0017](0017-no-radio-in-v1-trimmed-component-set.md)).
  - (−) A genuine licence hole remains open: two files with no redistribution grant sit in every binary we build, including every OTA image. It is bounded and plausibly inside the notice's purpose, but it is not resolved here — ADR 0004 or ADR 0012 must close it.
  - (−) The study **narrowed** open questions without closing them: H-slot (PDM slot mask) is still open with three contradictory vendor answers; the display pixel clock is still a bench item; the microphone's rail is *(prov.)* "an always-on 3.3 V rail, not any switchable LDO" until the schematic sheet is read. What was removed is wrong candidates, not uncertainty.
  - (−) Every claim here is a **snapshot**. SensorLib master ≠ 0.4.1 (the class we write against is a deprecation shim upstream), esp-dsp's clone carries no tags, LilyGoLib is a live tree. The notes must be re-read at each version bump, which is now a line the upgrade procedure has to carry.
  - (−) New Phase-1 work falls out of this: our own cycle-count harness (esp-dsp's published benchmarks are IRAM-resident via `test/linker.lf`, stop at 1024 complex points, and have **no row** for `bit_rev*`, `cplx2real` or any window), an audio-dropout counter fed by `on_recv_q_ovf`, and the upstream esp-dsp issue.
  - Flagged as a validation item: per-preset FFT-stage cycle counts on target (both `CONFIG_DSP_OPTIMIZED` settings, with and without an IRAM mapping) and the audio-dropout count per take, both reported in [`../validation/README.md`](../validation/README.md); the `### DSP metrics (per ADR 0018)` block is appended there when the harness exists, and the extrapolated figures in the esp-dsp note stay `(prov.)` until it does.
